# One-Way ANOVA

Testing whether several independent normal populations have equal expectations under the assumption of a common variance.

## Notebook contents

- formulation of the null and alternative hypotheses;
- calculation of between-group and within-group sums of squares;
- Fisher test statistic and its distribution;
- p-value calculation and decision at `alpha = 0.05`;
- three reproducible simulation examples;
- tables, visualizations and interpretation of the results.

## Run

Open `analysis.ipynb` in Jupyter Notebook or JupyterLab and run all cells.
